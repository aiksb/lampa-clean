# 🗺️ Lampa Clean Mirror - Roadmap

> Strategic vision for becoming the world's best online video viewing solution

## 🎯 Vision Statement

To create the most powerful, user-friendly, and privacy-respecting online video platform that combines the best features from multiple streaming solutions while maintaining simplicity and performance.
